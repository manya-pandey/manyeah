An AI app for origami: it recognizes models from photos and gathers the tutorials to fold them.

- Engineered a **CNN** model reaching **76% accuracy** on complex origami classification, prioritizing **real-world performance** and holding off overfitting with **strategic fine-tuning**.
- Deployed it as an app that **automates curation of tutorials** from YouTube and Pinterest, cutting the manual effort by **95%** with **ML and web scraping**.
- Shortened the project lifecycle with **transfer learning**: prototypes went from **4 weeks to 1.5**, training time dropped by **60%** and costs by **45%**.
