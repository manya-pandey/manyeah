---
title: Confidence Is Not Competence
status: preprint
venue: arXiv
date: 2025-10-01
link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.24772
---

Identifies a mechanistic disconnect between how confident large language models are and how well they actually reason. Working with **Llama 3.1**, **Mistral** and **Gemma 3**, the paper uses **linear probes**, **steering vectors** and **PCA**-based dimensionality analysis to look at how confidence and reasoning are represented inside the models, and finds that the two don't line up.
