Interpretability and low-resource NLP research alongside my degree at KIIT.

- Researching the **geometry of internal representations**: how a transformer's "assessment" of a problem and its "execution" of the solution come apart inside the model.
- Implementing **linear steering vectors** in PyTorch and Hugging Face to test their **causal influence** on model output. This line of work became the *Confidence Is Not Competence* paper.
- Supported the development of **NagaNLP**, helping **instruction-tune Llama-3.2** models with **Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA)** and building the synthetic data pipelines behind them.
