A year-long programme where Adobe picked about 100 students out of roughly 19,000, which is a 0.5% chance. I would love to say I planned for those odds.

The year opened with a day at Adobe's Noida campus: a fireside chat with Abhigyan Modi, a panel on AI and creative careers, and a look at unreleased products that I am contractually unable to describe. It was very good. You will have to trust me.
