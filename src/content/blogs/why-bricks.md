description: A short note on Augustus, marble, and guestbooks.

Suetonius has Augustus boasting that he found Rome a city of brick and left it a city of marble.

I liked the idea of a site that gets built a little by everyone who visits. So at the bottom of the homepage there's a button. Click it and you lay a brick. Five bricks stacked together turn to marble. The rest stay brick, waiting for company.
