A character-level language model built from scratch, to understand the probabilistic foundations underneath modern LLMs.

- Implemented the model **without high-level abstractions**, so every step of training and sampling is visible.
- Wrote **maximum likelihood estimation** and the **negative log-likelihood loss** by hand.
- Analyzed its **generation dynamics** and **token probability distributions** to see what a bigram model can and can't capture.
