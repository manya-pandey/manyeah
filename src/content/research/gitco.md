A novel context optimization framework that improves the inference-time capabilities of time series foundation models. A few structurally anomalous patches in a model's context can quietly drag down its forecasts; GITCO's Gate–Router–Critic design finds and suppresses them at inference time, with no retraining. On TimesFM 2.5 across 53 GIFT-Eval datasets it reduces average MASE by 1.95%, capturing 89.9% of the theoretical upper bound.

Accepted at the ICML **Foundation Models for Structured Data (FMSD)** and **GlobalSouth** workshops.
